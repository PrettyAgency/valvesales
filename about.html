About Valve Sales Australia

An Australian owned and operated valve and actuation supplier, part of the Pressform Group.

Who we are

Valves and actuation for energy, power and process

Valve Sales Australia was founded in 2008 and is part of Pressform Group, established in 1978. We represent specialist manufacturers and work from offices in Bassendean, Western Australia and Bongaree, Queensland.

Our sister company, Pressure Lube International, has provided on-site valve service since 2002.
