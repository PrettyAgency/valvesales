Gas-Over-Oil Actuators

Overview

Paladon GH and GHL series actuators use high-pressure process gas acting on hydraulic fluid in gas-over-oil tanks to drive the actuator.

Local open and close is standard, using either the process gas or a hydraulic hand pump.

Applications

  • Gas transmission lines

    Rotary and linear valves in gas transmission.

  • Sour and wet gas

    Suitable for sour and wet power gas.

Operating ranges and specifications

Supply pressure
13 to 250 barg (90 to 3,625 psig)
Torque output (GH)
200 to 680,000 Nm (1,770 to 6,018,000 lb in)
Thrust output (GHL)
Up to 289,134 N (65,000 lbf)
Low temperature (scotch-yoke)
Lloyds certified operation down to -65 C (-85 F)
Ingress protection
IP66M certified

Key features and benefits

  • SIL 3

    Third-party approved for SIL 3 applications.

  • Modular manifolds

    Compact manifolded controls for quick, low-cost changes or servicing.

  • Enclosed controls

    Fully enclosed with a lockable cover.

  • Hydraulic override

    Standard, for use if power gas is lost.

  • Speed control

    Independent open and close speed control.

  • Approved tanks

    PED or ASME approved gas-over-oil and power gas storage tanks.

  • Options

    Line-break detection and remote control.

Configurations and options

  • Scotch-yoke: symmetrical, canted, demi-canted
  • Linear systems (GHL)
  • Double-acting
  • Spring-return
  • Low or high pressure control systems
