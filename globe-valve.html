Manufacturer: Orion Valves

Overview

A cast steel, outside screw and yoke globe valve with a rising stem and a swivel plug disc. The backseat lets the gland be repacked under pressure.

Orion offers a linear-opening regulating disc for throttling and a stop-check version with an externally guided disc that is not connected to the stem.

Operating ranges and specifications

Standard
BS 1873 / API 623
Pressure classes
ASME 150 (PN 20), 300 (PN 50), 600 (PN 100), 900 (PN 150), 1500 (PN 250), 2500 (PN 420)
Body to bonnet joint
Male-and-female on Classes 150 and 300; ring joint on Class 600 and above
Disc guiding
Guided in the body from 4 in and up
Materials
Carbon or stainless steel; other corrosion resistant alloys on request
Disc and seat
Disc forged stainless to 8 in and cast steel above; flat, tapered, plug or regulating type

Key features and benefits

  • Repack under pressure

    The backseat bushing allows repacking without draining the valve.

  • Throttling option

    A linear-opening regulating disc is available.

  • Stop-check version

    Externally guided disc, disconnected from the stem.

  • Lining and cladding

    Wetted surfaces can be lined, or clad on larger sizes.
