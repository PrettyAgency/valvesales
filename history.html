Our history and values

Founded in 2008 and part of a group that began in 1978, with five values that guide how we work.

Company history

Founded in 2008

Founded in 2008, Valve Sales Australia (VSA) was immediately appointed the sole Australian distributor for MIR VALVE. VSA distributed Italian-designed, world-class valves manufactured locally in the Asia-Pacific region, responding to Australian demand for high-quality, locally made valves. Malaysia-based MIR Valve offered faster delivery at competitive prices.

In 2012, VSA expanded its product offering through additional agencies to better serve the energy sector, developing the capability to supply complete product offerings for greenfield and brownfield projects.

Key milestones

  1. 1978John Worner forms Pressform Engineering Pty Ltd.
  2. 2002Pressure Lube International Pty Ltd founded.
  3. 2008Valve Sales Australia Pty Ltd founded.
  4. 2012Additional product-line agencies introduced to broaden supply.
  5. 2018Jonathon Worner contracted as Group General Manager.
  6. 2020Corey Clifton contracted as General Manager.
